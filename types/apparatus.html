Content of type 'apparatus'

People

Prosopographical register of geometers, philosophers, commentators, and editors appearing in Proclus’s Commentary on the First Book of Euclid’s Elements (including all twenty-three geometers of the Eudemian Summary, Friedlein pp. 64–70) and in the textual transmission of the Codex.

Bibliography

Critical register of primary editions, translations, ancient mathematical authorities cited by Proclus, and modern GEOMETOR companion treatises. Every :cite: reference in the Codex resolves directly to its entry below.

Friedlein Citation Concordance

Complete bidirectional concordance mapping every passage of Proclus’s Commentary on the First Book of Euclid’s Elements (ed. Gottfried Friedlein, Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1873) cited across GEOMETOR (euclid and hoenen) — 164 canonical Friedlein loci encompassing 156 distinct euclid citation keys (159 citing occurrences across Heath Vols. I–III) and 10 philosophical-epistemological loci in Hoenen’s De Noetica Geometriae.

Glossary

Lexicon of Greek mathematical, logical, and Neoplatonic epistemological terminology in Proclus’s Commentary on Book I, cross-referenced with Euclid’s Elements and Petrus Hoenen’s Scholastic-noetic vocabulary.
