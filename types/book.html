Commentary on the First Book of Euclid’s Elements

Composed at the Neoplatonic Academy of Athens around 470 AD, Proclus Diadochus's Commentary on the First Book of Euclid’s Elements is the supreme surviving monument of ancient mathematical philosophy, axiomatic methodology, and the pre-Euclidean history of Greek geometry. Each unit of this Multi-Track Book Codex provides four synchronized reading tracks: English, Greek, Summary, and Notes.
