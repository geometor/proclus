geometor.proclus¶

GEOMETOR Proclus: Multi-Track Book Codex and Friedlein Citation Concordance.

Scholarly digital reconstruction and apparatus for Proclus Diadochus’s In Primum Euclidis Elementorum Librum Commentarii (Commentary on the First Book of Euclid’s Elements, c. 470 AD), grounded in Gottfried Friedlein’s 1873 Teubner Greek edition and Thomas Taylor’s 1788–1789/1792 English translation, and cross-linked with geometor/euclid, geometor/elements, and geometor/hoenen.

geometor.proclus.parse_friedlein_locus(locus: str, display_hint: str = '') → FriedleinLocus¶

Parse a Friedlein (1873) page/line locus key into structured page and line bounds.

Handles standard Teubner citations ("12.14-22", "103.21-107.10"), whole-page ranges ("64-70", "399-400"), compressed trailing-digit page ranges ("303-4", "323-6", "424-5"), and Hoenen/Heath shorthand keys ("p97", "p185", "65-20", "189.190").
