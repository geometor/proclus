Postulate 4: Equality of All Right Angles — GEOMETOR Commentary & Concordance Notes
===================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

:just:`Postulate 4 <I.post.4>` (*all right angles are equal*) reflects the determinacy of perpendicularity; Proclus also records :person:`Pappus's <pappus>` acute observation (*Friedlein* 189–190) that the converse fails if curvilinear lunular angles are admitted.

Friedlein Loci Cited in GEOMETOR (2 Loci in pp. 188–191)
--------------------------------------------------------

* :ref:`Friedlein 189–190 <conc-friedlein-189-190>` (:ref:`text <sec-post-4>`) — Discussion of Postulate 4 and Pappus's remark that the converse ('an angle equal to a right angle is not necessarily right' if curvilinear, e.g. lunular angle) fails.
  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:189.190``): Heath Vol. I, p. 25 [folio ``0039``]
* :ref:`Friedlein 189, 11 <conc-friedlein-189-11>` (:ref:`text <sec-post-4>`) — Geminus's attempted proof of Postulate 4 (that all right angles are equal) from the uniformity of right-angled inclination.
  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:189.11``): Heath Vol. I, p. 201 [folio ``0215``]
