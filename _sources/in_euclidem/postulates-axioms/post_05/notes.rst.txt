Postulate 5: The Parallel Postulate & Asymptotic Curves — GEOMETOR Commentary & Concordance Notes
=================================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

In one of the most consequential passages in mathematical history, Proclus declares that :just:`Postulate 5 <I.post.5>` *ought to be struck completely from the postulates* because it lacks self-evidence, its converse (:just:`Prop. I.17 <I.17>`) is a proved theorem, and asymptotic curves prove that continuous convergence alone does not entail intersection.

Friedlein Loci Cited in GEOMETOR (2 Loci in pp. 191–193)
--------------------------------------------------------

* :ref:`Friedlein 191, 21 <conc-friedlein-191-21>` (:ref:`text <sec-post-5>`) — Historical testimony that from the outset geometers attacked Postulate 5 as lacking self-evidence and sought either to prove it or redefine parallels.
