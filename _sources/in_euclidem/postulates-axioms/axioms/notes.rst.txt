Common Notions (Axioms 1–5): Apollonius, Heron & Pappus — GEOMETOR Commentary & Concordance Notes
=================================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

Proclus refutes :person:`Apollonius's <apollonius>` circular attempt to prove :just:`Common Notion 1 <I.cn.1>`, rejects scholiastic axiom inflation (such as *"two straight lines do not enclose a space"*), and compares :person:`Heron's <heron>` three-axiom canon with Euclid's five.

Friedlein Loci Cited in GEOMETOR (5 Loci in pp. 193–198)
--------------------------------------------------------

* :ref:`Friedlein 194, 8 <conc-friedlein-194-8>` (:ref:`text <sec-axioms>`) — Apollonius's attempted demonstration of Common Notion 1 ('things equal to the same are equal to one another') via a middle space/coincidence, and Proclus's refutation.
  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:194.8``): Heath Vol. I, p. 221 [folio ``0235``]
  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:194.8``): Heath Vol. I, p. 121 [folio ``0135``]
* :ref:`Friedlein 194, 10 <conc-friedlein-194-10>` (:ref:`text <sec-axioms>`) — Critique of proving axioms through things less evident than the axioms themselves.
  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:194.10``): Heath Vol. I, p. 62 [folio ``0076``]
* :ref:`Friedlein 196, 21 <conc-friedlein-196-21>` (:ref:`text <sec-axioms>`) — Rejection of the interpolated axiom 'two straight lines do not enclose a space' as belonging to the special matter of geometry rather than common notions.
  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:196.21``): Heath Vol. I, p. 232 [folio ``0246``]
* :ref:`Friedlein 197, 6–198, 5 <conc-friedlein-197-6-198-5>` (:ref:`text <sec-axioms>`) — Heron's restriction of authentic Euclidean Common Notions to three, and Pappus's proposed additional axioms.
  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:197.6-198.5``): Heath Vol. I, p. 224 [folio ``0238``]
* :ref:`Friedlein 198, 5–15 <conc-friedlein-198-5-15>` (:ref:`text <sec-axioms>`) — Editorial criterion separating Euclid's genuine five (or three) Common Notions from later scholiastic interpolations.
  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:198.5-15``): Heath Vol. I, p. 45 [folio ``0059``]
