Def. 23: Parallel Straight Lines (Euclid, Posidonius & Geminus) — GEOMETOR Commentary & Concordance Notes
=========================================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

Proclus contrasts Euclid's coplanar non-intersection definition of parallels (:just:`I.def.23`) with :person:`Posidonius's <posidonius>` equidistance definition, preserving :person:`Geminus's <geminus>` profound warning on asymptotic curves (*Friedlein* 176–177).

* :ref:`Friedlein 176, 5–17 <conc-friedlein-176-5-17>` (:ref:`text <sec-def-23>`) — Posidonius's equidistant definition of parallel lines contrasted with Euclid's non-secant coplanar definition; excerpt from Geminus.
  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:176.5-17``): Heath Vol. I, p. 40 [folio ``0054``]
* :ref:`Friedlein 176, 6–17 <conc-friedlein-176-6-17>` (:ref:`text <sec-def-23>`) — Critique of defining parallels by equidistance: non-intersecting lines vs. equidistant loci.

* :ref:`Friedlein 177, 11 <conc-friedlein-177-11>` (:ref:`text <sec-def-23>`) — Geminus's crucial warning on asymptotic curves (hyperbola and conchoid of Nicomedes): lines can converge continually forever without ever meeting.
