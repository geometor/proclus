Def. 4: Straight Line (Eutheia) & Spiric/Conic Classifications — Analytical Summary
===================================================================================

*Scope*: **Friedlein 109–113** · *Euclid*: :just:`I.def.4`

Analytical Synopsis
-------------------

Proclus harmonizes :just:`I.def.4` (*"lies evenly with the points on itself"*) with :person:`Plato's <plato>` optical definition (:cite:`Parmenides 137e <plato:parmenides:137e>`) and :person:`Archimedes' <archimedes>` shortest-distance axiom (*Friedlein* 109–110), and records :person:`Perseus's <perseus>` spiric sections (*Friedlein* 111–112).
