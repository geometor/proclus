Defs. 5–7: Surface (Epiphaneia), Boundaries & Plane Surface — GEOMETOR Commentary & Concordance Notes
=====================================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

Proclus traces two-dimensional magnitude (:just:`I.def.5`–:just:`I.def.7`), its linear boundaries, and planar flatness in parallel with rectilinear evenness, contrasting :person:`Plato's <plato>` and :person:`Aristotle's <aristotle>` treatment of plane geometry with Euclid's.
