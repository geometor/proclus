Commentary on the Definitions (I.Def.1–23)
==========================================

Across ninety-three pages (**Friedlein pp. 85–177**; Taylor Book II, Definitions I–XXXV), :person:`Proclus <proclus>` expounds :person:`Euclid's <euclid>` twenty-three Book I Definitions (:just:`I.def.1`–:just:`I.def.23`) as a systematic descent from indivisible unity into planar figure. Each section below presents four synchronized tracks: **English**, **Greek (1873)**, **Summary**, and **Notes**.
