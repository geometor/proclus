Def. 1: Point (Semeion) as Indivisible Limit with Position — Analytical Summary
===============================================================================

*Scope*: **Friedlein 85–96** · *Euclid*: :just:`I.def.1`

Analytical Synopsis
-------------------

Proclus contrasts Euclid's negative definition (:just:`I.def.1`, *"that which has no part"*) with the Pythagorean positive definition (*"a monad having position"*, *Friedlein* 95, 21), showing why negative characterization befits the first limit of magnitude.
