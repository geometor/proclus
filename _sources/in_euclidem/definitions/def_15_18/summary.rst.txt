Defs. 15–18: Circle, Center, Diameter (Thales) & Semicircle — Analytical Summary
================================================================================

*Scope*: **Friedlein 146–159** · *Euclid*: :just:`I.def.15`

Analytical Synopsis
-------------------

Proclus celebrates the circle (:just:`I.def.15`–:just:`I.def.18`) as the first and fairest of plane figures, distinguishes its interior center from poles outside the plane, and records :person:`Thales's <thales>` first demonstration that the diameter bisects the circle (*Friedlein* 157, 10).
