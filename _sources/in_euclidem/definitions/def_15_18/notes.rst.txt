Defs. 15–18: Circle, Center, Diameter (Thales) & Semicircle — GEOMETOR Commentary & Concordance Notes
=====================================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

Proclus celebrates the circle (:just:`I.def.15`–:just:`I.def.18`) as the first and fairest of plane figures, distinguishes its interior center from poles outside the plane, and records :person:`Thales's <thales>` first demonstration that the diameter bisects the circle (*Friedlein* 157, 10).

  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:153.3``): Heath Vol. I, p. 185 [folio ``0199``]
* :ref:`Friedlein 157, 10 <conc-friedlein-157-10>` (:ref:`text <sec-def-15-18>`) — Commentary on Def. 17 (Diameter): Thales's first demonstration (recorded by Eudemus) that the diameter bisects the circle.
