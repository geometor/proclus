Defs. 13–14: Boundary (Horos), Figure (Schema) & Protagoras on Tangency — GEOMETOR Commentary & Concordance Notes
=================================================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

Proclus explains :term:`boundary <horos>` (:just:`I.def.13`), refutes :person:`Protagoras's <protagoras>` empiricist attack on circle-line tangency (*Friedlein* 139–140), and contrasts Euclid's contained-space figure (:just:`I.def.14`) with :person:`Posidonius's <posidonius>` enclosing-perimeter figure (*Friedlein* 143).

Friedlein Loci Cited in GEOMETOR (3 Loci in pp. 136–146)
--------------------------------------------------------

* :ref:`Friedlein 139, 26–140, 7 <conc-friedlein-139-26-140-7>` (:ref:`text <sec-def-13-14>`) — Refutation of Protagoras's empiricist objection that a circle touches a straight ruler across a sensible segment rather than at an indivisible point.
  - ``hoenen`` (``proclus:in-euclid:12.14-22``): *De Noetica Geometriae* Caput III, p. 67
* :ref:`Friedlein 140, 4 <conc-friedlein-140-4>` (:ref:`text <sec-def-13-14>`) — Exactitude of intelligible tangency at a single point versus the coarse imperfection of sensible rulers and drawn circles.
  - ``hoenen`` (``proclus:in-euclid:12.14``): *De Noetica Geometriae* Caput III, p. 67
* :ref:`Friedlein 143, 8 <conc-friedlein-143-8>` (:ref:`text <sec-def-13-14>`) — Comparison of Euclid's definition of Figure (Def. 14) with Posidonius's definition ('enclosing limit'), explaining why Euclid includes the contained interior.
  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:143.8``): Heath Vol. I, p. 183 [folio ``0197``]
