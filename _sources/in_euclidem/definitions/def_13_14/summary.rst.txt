Defs. 13–14: Boundary (Horos), Figure (Schema) & Protagoras on Tangency — Analytical Summary
============================================================================================

*Scope*: **Friedlein 136–146** · *Euclid*: :just:`I.def.14`

Analytical Synopsis
-------------------

Proclus explains :term:`boundary <horos>` (:just:`I.def.13`), refutes :person:`Protagoras's <protagoras>` empiricist attack on circle-line tangency (*Friedlein* 139–140), and contrasts Euclid's contained-space figure (:just:`I.def.14`) with :person:`Posidonius's <posidonius>` enclosing-perimeter figure (*Friedlein* 143).
