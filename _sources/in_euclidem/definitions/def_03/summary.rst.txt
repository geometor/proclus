Def. 3: Extremities of a Line & Homoeomeric Lines — Analytical Summary
======================================================================

*Scope*: **Friedlein 103–109** · *Euclid*: :just:`I.def.3`

Analytical Synopsis
-------------------

Proclus analyzes how points bound a line (:just:`I.def.3`) and preserves :person:`Geminus's <geminus>` classification of curves—proving (*Friedlein* 105) that only three curves are :term:`homoeomeric <homoiomeres>` (straight line, circle, cylindrical helix).
