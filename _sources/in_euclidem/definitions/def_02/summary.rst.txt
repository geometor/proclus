Def. 2: Line (Gramme), Flux of a Point & Classification of Curves — Analytical Summary
======================================================================================

*Scope*: **Friedlein 96–103** · *Euclid*: :just:`I.def.2`

Analytical Synopsis
-------------------

Alongside :just:`I.def.2` (*"breadthless length"*), Proclus examines the dynamic generation of the line as the :term:`flux of a point <rhysis>` (*Friedlein* 97, 6–13) and contrasts it with the Pythagorean correspondence of point, line, surface, and solid to monad, dyad, triad, and tetrad.
