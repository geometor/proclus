Defs. 8–9: Plane Angle, Category of Angle & Horn Angles (Keratoeides) — Analytical Summary
==========================================================================================

*Scope*: **Friedlein 121–131** · *Euclid*: :just:`I.def.8`

Analytical Synopsis
-------------------

Proclus preserves the celebrated debate of :person:`Eudemus <eudemus>`, :person:`Carpus of Antioch <carpus>`, :person:`Plutarch of Athens <plutarch-of-athens>`, and :person:`Syrianus <syrianus>` over whether an angle (:just:`I.def.8`–:just:`I.def.9`) belongs to *Relation*, *Quality*, or *Quantity*—tested by the non-Archimedean :term:`horn angle <keratoeides>` (*Friedlein* 121–127).
