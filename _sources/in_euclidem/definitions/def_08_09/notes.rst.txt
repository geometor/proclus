Defs. 8–9: Plane Angle, Category of Angle & Horn Angles (Keratoeides) — GEOMETOR Commentary & Concordance Notes
===============================================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

Proclus preserves the celebrated debate of :person:`Eudemus <eudemus>`, :person:`Carpus of Antioch <carpus>`, :person:`Plutarch of Athens <plutarch-of-athens>`, and :person:`Syrianus <syrianus>` over whether an angle (:just:`I.def.8`–:just:`I.def.9`) belongs to *Relation*, *Quality*, or *Quantity*—tested by the non-Archimedean :term:`horn angle <keratoeides>` (*Friedlein* 121–127).

Friedlein Loci Cited in GEOMETOR (3 Loci in pp. 121–131)
--------------------------------------------------------

* :ref:`Friedlein 121, 24–122, 6 <conc-friedlein-121-24-122-6>` (:ref:`text <sec-def-08-09>`) — Ancient debate on the ontological category of the angle (relation, quality, or quantity) and the paradox of the horn angle (keratoeides).
  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:121-24-122-6``): Heath Vol. II, p. 40 [folio ``0046``]
* :ref:`Friedlein 123, 16 <conc-friedlein-123-16>` (:ref:`text <sec-def-08-09>`) — Classification of plane and solid angles and the inclination of planes.
  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:123.16``): Heath Vol. III, p. 264 [folio ``0268``]
* :ref:`Friedlein 126, 7–127, 16 <conc-friedlein-126-7-127-16>` (:ref:`text <sec-def-08-09>`) — Geminus's exhaustive combinatorics of plane angles formed by straight lines, circles, cissoids, hippopedes, and conchoids (rectilinear, lunular, horn-like, mixed).
  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:126.7-127.16``): Heath Vol. I, p. 178 [folio ``0192``]
