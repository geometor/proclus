Prop. I.21: Interior Triangle Segments and Greater Enclosed Angle — GEOMETOR Commentary & Concordance Notes
===========================================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

Proclus's commentary on :just:`Prop. I.21 <I.21>` (*Friedlein* pp. 326–329) forms an essential link in Book I's systematic construction of plane geometry.
