Prop. I.35: Parallelograms on the Same Base & Birth of Locus Theorems — GEOMETOR Commentary & Concordance Notes
===============================================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

Proclus's commentary on :just:`Prop. I.35 <I.35>` (*Friedlein* pp. 394–400) forms an essential link in Book I's systematic construction of plane geometry.
