Prop. I.22: Construction of a Triangle from Three Segments — GEOMETOR Commentary & Concordance Notes
====================================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

Proclus's commentary on :just:`Prop. I.22 <I.22>` (*Friedlein* pp. 330–333) forms an essential link in Book I's systematic construction of plane geometry.
