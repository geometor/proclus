Prop. I.1: Construction of the Equilateral Triangle — GEOMETOR Commentary & Concordance Notes
=============================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

Proclus's commentary on :just:`Prop. I.1 <I.1>` (*Friedlein* pp. 208–219) forms an essential link in Book I's systematic construction of plane geometry.
