Prop. I.26: Congruence by ASA and AAS & Thales' Ship Distance Theorem — GEOMETOR Commentary & Concordance Notes
===============================================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

Proclus's commentary on :just:`Prop. I.26 <I.26>` (*Friedlein* pp. 347–353) forms an essential link in Book I's systematic construction of plane geometry.

Friedlein Loci Cited in GEOMETOR (3 Loci in pp. 347–353)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

* :ref:`Friedlein 345–347 <conc-friedlein-345-347>` (:ref:`text <sec-prop-26>`) — Commentary on Prop. I.25: direct (non-reductio) alternative demonstrations of the converse Hinge Theorem by Menelaus of Alexandria and Heron.
  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:345-7``): Heath Vol. I, p. 300 (Bk. I, propositions) [folio ``0314``]
* :ref:`Friedlein 351 <conc-friedlein-351>` (:ref:`text <sec-prop-26>`) — Commentary on Prop. I.26: Porphyry's and Eudemus's exhaustive combinatorial audit of the six possible three-part triangle congruence hypotheses (why SSA and AAA fail).
  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:351``): Heath Vol. I, p. 306 (Bk. I, propositions) [folio ``0320``]
* :ref:`Friedlein 352, 14–18 <conc-friedlein-352-14-18>` (:ref:`text <sec-prop-26>`) — Eudemus's famous report in his History of Geometry attributing Prop. I.26 to Thales, who must have used it in his method of finding the distance of ships at sea.
  - ``euclid`` (``proclus:in-euclid:352.14-18``): Heath Vol. I, p. 36 (chapter_iv) [folio ``0050``]
