Prop. I.29: Parallel Lines & Proclus's Attempted Proof of Postulate 5 — GEOMETOR Commentary & Concordance Notes
===============================================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

Proclus's commentary on :just:`Prop. I.29 <I.29>` (*Friedlein* pp. 364–373) forms an essential link in Book I's systematic construction of plane geometry.
