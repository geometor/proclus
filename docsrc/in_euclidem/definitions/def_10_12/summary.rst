Defs. 10–12: Right Angle, Perpendicular, Obtuse & Acute Angles — Analytical Summary
===================================================================================

*Scope*: **Friedlein 131–136** · *Euclid*: :just:`I.def.10`

Analytical Synopsis
-------------------

In :just:`I.def.10`–:just:`I.def.12`, the right angle embodies :term:`Limit <peras>`, determinacy, and equality, whereas obtuse and acute angles embody :term:`the Unlimited <apeiron>` through indefinite excess and defect.
