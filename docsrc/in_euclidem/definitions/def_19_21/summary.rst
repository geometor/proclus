Defs. 19–21: Rectilinear Figures & Sevenfold Species of Triangles — Analytical Summary
======================================================================================

*Scope*: **Friedlein 159–168** · *Euclid*: :just:`I.def.20`

Analytical Synopsis
-------------------

Proclus examines rectilinear figures (:just:`I.def.19`–:just:`I.def.21`) and proves combinatorially why there are **seven** (not nine) side-angle species of triangles (*Friedlein* 165, 24).
