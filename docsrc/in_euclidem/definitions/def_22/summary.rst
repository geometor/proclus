Def. 22: Quadrilaterals (Square, Oblong, Rhombus, Rhomboid, Trapezia) — Analytical Summary
==========================================================================================

*Scope*: **Friedlein 169–175** · *Euclid*: :just:`I.def.22`

Analytical Synopsis
-------------------

Proclus organizes :person:`Posidonius's <posidonius>` sevenfold taxonomy of quadrilaterals (:just:`I.def.22`): square, oblong, rhombus, rhomboid, isosceles trapezium, scalene trapezium, and trapezoid (*Friedlein* 169–174).
