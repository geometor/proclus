====================================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

Proclus's commentary on :just:`Prop. I.5 <I.5>` (*Friedlein* pp. 244–251) forms an essential link in Book I's systematic construction of plane geometry.
