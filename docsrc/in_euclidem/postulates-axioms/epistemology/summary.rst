Distinction of Hypotheses, Postulates & Axioms — Analytical Summary
===================================================================

*Scope*: **Friedlein 178–184**

Analytical Synopsis
-------------------

Reviewing :person:`Speusippus <speusippus>`, :person:`Amphinomus <amphinomus>`, :person:`Geminus <geminus>`, and :person:`Aristotle <aristotle>` (:cite:`Posterior Analytics I.10 <aristotle:anal-post:I.1-10>`), Proclus distinguishes :term:`axioms <axioma>` (self-evident cognitive truths common across sciences) from :term:`postulates <aitema>` (constructive geometric demands easily granted without complex machinery).
