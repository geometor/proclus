Postulates 1–3: Straightedge & Compass Constructibility (Geminus) — Analytical Summary
======================================================================================

*Scope*: **Friedlein 185–187** · *Euclid*: :just:`I.post.1`

Analytical Synopsis
-------------------

:just:`Postulates 1–3 <I.post.1>` ground the entire constructive universe of straightedge and compass in the uniform, undeviating :term:`flux <rhysis>` of a point (quoting :person:`Geminus <geminus>` at *Friedlein* 185) and the revolution of a bounded distance around a stationary center.
