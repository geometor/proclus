Postulates 1–3: Straightedge & Compass Constructibility (Geminus) — GEOMETOR Commentary & Concordance Notes
===========================================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

:just:`Postulates 1–3 <I.post.1>` ground the entire constructive universe of straightedge and compass in the uniform, undeviating :term:`flux <rhysis>` of a point (quoting :person:`Geminus <geminus>` at *Friedlein* 185) and the revolution of a bounded distance around a stationary center.

Friedlein Loci Cited in GEOMETOR (1 Loci in pp. 185–187)
--------------------------------------------------------

* :ref:`Friedlein 185 <conc-friedlein-185>` (:ref:`text <sec-post-1-3>`) — Commentary on Postulate 1 quoting Geminus in Greek: because the line is the flux of a point and the straight line a uniform undeviating flux, joining two points is postulated.
  - ``hoenen`` (``proclus:in-euclid:p185``): *De Noetica Geometriae* Caput IV, §4, p. 121
