===================================================

Bridging Book I's Definitions and its forty-eight Propositions (**Friedlein pp. 178–207**; Taylor Book III opening), Proclus establishes both the **epistemology of unproved geometric principles** and the **formal logic of Euclidean demonstration**. Each section below presents four synchronized tracks: **English**, **Greek (1873)**, **Summary**, and **Notes**.
