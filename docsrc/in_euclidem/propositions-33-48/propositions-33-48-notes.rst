Commentary on Propositions I.33–I.48: Areas, Application & Pythagoras — GEOMETOR Commentary & Concordance Notes
===============================================================================================================

GEOMETOR Philosophical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------------------

1. **Locus Theorems (`Friedlein 394–396`) & Textual Lacuna at `I.36`–`I.37` (`Friedlein 403, 5`)**:
   Proclus's recognition of :just:`Prop. I.35 <I.35>` as the prototype of **locus theorems** (:term:`τοπικὰ θεωρήματα <topikon-theorema>`) links elementary area-shearing to higher Greek locus geometry (:person:`Hermotimus <hermotimus>`, Aristaeus, :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>`). Note at `Friedlein 403, 5` (cited by :cite:`Heath <heath:1926>`, Vol. I, p. 332) a famous manuscript lacuna spanning the end of Proclus's note on :just:`I.36 <I.36>` and the opening of :just:`I.37 <I.37>`, present in all extant Greek codices and noted by :person:`Barocius <barocius>` and :person:`Friedlein <friedlein>`. (Also note Taylor's 1792 heading misprint `PROPOSITION LXVIII` for `XLVIII`, normalized in our heading.)
2. **Application of Areas (`Friedlein 419–420`) & The Golden Section**:
   Proclus's preservation of :person:`Eudemus's <eudemus>` report on :just:`Prop. I.44 <I.44>`—that :term:`parabole <parabole>`, :term:`hyperbole <hyperbole>`, and :term:`elleipsis <elleipsis>` belong to the ancient Pythagorean muse—unlocks the unity of Greek geometry:
   - Exact application (*parabole*, :just:`I.44 <I.44>`–:just:`I.45 <I.45>`) solves $ax = S$ geometrically via the gnomon complement (:just:`I.43 <I.43>`).
   - Application with square excess (*hyperbole*) in :just:`II.11 <II.11>` and :just:`VI.30 <VI.30>` ($x(a + x) = a^2$) constructs **division in extreme and mean ratio**—the **Divine Proportion** $\phi$ at the heart of **GEOMETOR**!
3. **Euclid's Proof of `I.47` & Pythagorean Triples (`Friedlein 426–429`)**:
   Proclus is our sole ancient source crediting **Euclid personally** with the area-shearing Book I proof of :just:`Prop. I.47 <I.47>` (necessary because Book V's Eudoxian proportion theory has not yet been introduced in Book I!) as well as recording side-by-side the **Pythagorean odd generator** $(2k+1,\; 2k^2+2k,\; 2k^2+2k+1)$ and the **Platonic even generator** $(2m,\; m^2-1,\; m^2+1)$.
