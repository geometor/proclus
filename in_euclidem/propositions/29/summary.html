Prop. I.29: Parallel Lines & Proclus’s Attempted Proof of Postulate 5 — Analytical Summary¶

Scope: Friedlein pp. 364–373 · Book I Proposition 29 · Euclid: [I.29]

Architectural Role in Book I¶

In Proposition I.29 (Friedlein pp. 364–373), Proclus treats Euclid’s enunciation of Prop. I.29, examining its formal structure within the axiomatic hierarchy of the Elements, its methodological status as a geometrical theorem, and its Neoplatonic-cosmological significance.
