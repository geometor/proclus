Prop. I.23: Constructing an Angle Equal to a Given Angle (Oenopides) — Analytical Summary¶

Scope: Friedlein pp. 333–335 · Book I Proposition 23 · Euclid: [I.23]

Architectural Role in Book I¶

In Proposition I.23 (Friedlein pp. 333–335), Proclus treats Euclid’s enunciation of Prop. I.23, examining its formal structure within the axiomatic hierarchy of the Elements, its methodological status as a geometrical problem, and its Neoplatonic-cosmological significance.
