Commentary on the First Book of Euclid’s Elements¶

Εἰς τὸ αʹ τῶν Εὐκλείδου Στοιχείων (In Primum Euclidis Elementorum Librum Commentarii) — Proclus Diadochus

ΠΡΟΚΛΟΥ ΔΙΑΔΟΧΟΥ
ΕΙΣ ΤΟ ΠΡΩΤΟΝ ΤΩΝ ΕΥΚΛΕΙΔΟΥ ΣΤΟΙΧΕΙΩΝ
In Primum Euclidis Elementorum Librum Commentarii

Author: Proclus Lycius Diadochus (412–485 AD)
Critical Greek Edition: Gottfried Friedlein (Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1873)
English Translation: Thomas Taylor (London, 1788–1789 / 1792) & GEOMETOR Scholarly Apparatus
GEOMETOR Digital Edition: Synchronized English, Greek (1873), Summary & Notes Tracks with Friedlein Folio Anchors

Table of Contents¶

Composed at the Neoplatonic Academy of Athens around 470 AD, Proclus Diadochus's Commentary on the First Book of Euclid’s Elements is the supreme surviving monument of ancient mathematical philosophy, axiomatic methodology, and the pre-Euclidean history of Greek geometry. Each unit of this Multi-Track Book Codex provides four synchronized reading tracks: English, Greek (1873), Summary, and Notes.

  • Prologue I (Friedlein pp. 3–47): Prologue Part I: Mathematical Being & Common Science — Intermediate Being (Metaxy), Limit & Unlimited, Common Mathematical Principles, Critique of Abstraction, and the Ascent to Dialectic. (EN · EL · Summary · Notes)

  • Prologue II (Friedlein pp. 48–84): Prologue Part II: Philosophy & History of Geometry — Mathematical Imagination (Phantasia & Probole), Intelligible Matter, The Eudemian Summary of Greek Geometry, and the Purpose of the Elements. (EN · EL · Summary · Notes)

  • Definitions (Friedlein pp. 85–177): Commentary on the Definitions (I.Def.1–23) — Point, Line & Flux, Straight Line, Surface, Plane & Horn Angles, Figure, Circle, Triangles, Quadrilaterals, and Parallel Lines. (EN · EL · Summary · Notes)

  • Postulates & Axioms (Friedlein pp. 178–207): Hypotheses, Postulates, Axioms & Propositional Form — Epistemology of First Principles, Compass & Straightedge Postulates, Critique of Postulate 5, Common Notions, and the Six Parts of a Proposition. (EN · EL · Summary · Notes)

  • Propositions I.1–I.15 (Friedlein pp. 208–305): Commentary on Propositions I.1–I.15: Triangles, Congruence & Perpendiculars — Equilateral Construction & Zeno’s Critique, Superposition (I.4), Pappus’s Proof of I.5, Converses & Reductio, Oenopides’ Gnomon (I.12), Vertical Angles & Porisms. (EN · EL · Summary · Notes)

  • Propositions I.16–I.32 (Friedlein pp. 305–384): Commentary on Propositions I.16–I.32: Inequalities, Parallels & Angle-Sum — Exterior Angle, Epicurean Critique of I.20, Thales’ Ship-Distance Theorem (I.26), Ptolemy & Proclus on Postulate 5 (I.29), and the Pythagorean Angle-Sum Proof (I.32). (EN · EL · Summary · Notes)

  • Propositions I.33–I.48 (Friedlein pp. 384–436): Commentary on Propositions I.33–I.48: Areas, Application & Pythagoras — Parallelograms & Locus Theorems (I.35), Pythagorean Application of Areas (Parabole, Hyperbole, Elleipsis, I.44), and Euclid’s Proof of I.47–I.48. (EN · EL · Summary · Notes)
