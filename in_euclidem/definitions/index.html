Commentary on the Definitions (I.Def.1–23)¶

Across ninety-three pages (Friedlein pp. 85–177; Taylor Book II, Definitions I–XXXV), Proclus expounds Euclid's twenty-three Book I Definitions ([I.def.1]–[I.def.23]) as a systematic descent from indivisible unity into planar figure. Each section below presents four synchronized tracks: English, Greek (1873), Summary, and Notes.
